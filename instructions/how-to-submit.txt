How to submit
The FE Bar has two independent submission steps on two separate tabs. Complete both - in either order - to finish the bar.

Submission - Build tab: upload your build artifacts and narrative, check the attestation, and submit. The evaluator returns a score and detailed per-domain feedback within a few minutes.
Submission - AI role-play tab: launch the Yoodli role-play and deliver your demo to the two AI personas (business and technical stakeholder). The session is scored automatically when it finishes.
Both steps are required. Either can be done first; they are independent of each other.
